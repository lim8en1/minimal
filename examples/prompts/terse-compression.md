Compress the conversation above into a working brief. Be ruthlessly short —
every token here is re-read on every future turn.

One heading per topic, nothing else:

- **Ask**: what the user wants, including every change to the ask.
- **State**: files touched (paths), commands run and their outcomes,
  decisions made and why — rejected approaches included.
- **Live wires**: what is running, broken, or half-done right now, and the
  exact next step for each.
- **Verbatim**: error messages, identifiers, and values that must survive
  unchanged. Copy them exactly or drop them.

No prose, no transitions, no summary of the summary. If two bullets can
become one, make them one.
